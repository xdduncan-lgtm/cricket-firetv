# Darts Scoreboard – Privacy Policy

Darts Scoreboard does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information.

- The app has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.
- Player names, game settings and games in progress are saved only on your own device, so a game can be resumed. They never leave the device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
- The app does not use the internet, camera, microphone or location.

Questions: contact the developer through the Amazon Appstore listing.
